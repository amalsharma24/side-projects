## 2. The Strategic Renewal Math

In your actual usage pattern:
- You valued **checked bags** at **\$270/yr** (6 flights $\times$ \$45).
- You used **2 lounge passes** valued at **\$30**.
- You used **1 instant delay credit** valued at **\$50**.
- You got **\$0** from the companion award.
- By putting \$996 of Alaska flights on the card to unlock the free bags, you incur a **-\$58 opportunity loss** vs. putting those flights on Amex Platinum (5X = 10%).

### Comparison Table:

$$\begin{aligned}
\text{Net ROI (Summit \$395)} &= (\$270 \text{ bags} + \$30 \text{ lounge} + \$50 \text{ delay}) - \$395 \text{ fee} - \$58 \text{ flight toll} + \$24 \text{ foreign edge} \\
&= \$350 - \$395 - \$34 \\
&= \mathbf{-\$79\text{/yr (Net Deficit)}}
\end{aligned}$$

$$\begin{aligned}
\text{Net ROI (Ascent \$95)} &= (\$270 \text{ bags} + \$0 \text{ inflight}) - \$95 \text{ fee} - \$58 \text{ flight toll} \\
&= \$270 - \$95 - \$58 \\
&= \mathbf{+\$117\text{/yr (Strong Net Positive!)}}
\end{aligned}$$

---

## 3. Clear Recommendation

1. **Downgrading from Summit to Ascent is a \$196/year net improvement** to your wallet (from -\$79 to +\$117).
2. The \$95 Ascent card retains the essential high-dollar perk you actually use: **free checked bags for you and your travel companions**.
3. It eliminates the \$300 excess fee for lounge passes that expire every 90 days and companion awards you did not redeem.
